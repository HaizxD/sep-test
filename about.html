OUR STORY

BUILDING THE
AI FUTURE

From a small group of curious students to a thriving innovation community — this is our journey.

The AI & Innovation Club was founded with a simple but powerful vision: to create a space where students from any background could come together to explore, learn and build with Artificial Intelligence.

What started as a small meetup of 10 students has grown into a diverse community of over 120 members — including data scientists, software engineers, designers, product managers and AI enthusiasts. We've partnered with leading tech companies, hosted industry workshops and built projects that have been featured at university showcases and innovation conferences.

Our club is built on the belief that AI is for everyone. You don't need a PhD in computer science to join us. You just need curiosity, creativity and a willingness to learn. Whether you're a complete beginner or already building your own models, there's a place for you here.
